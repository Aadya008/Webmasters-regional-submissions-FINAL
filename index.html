Learn how AI really works.

Nexus Citadel is a castle-themed course about artificial intelligence (AI). The course has three wings and eight chambers. Each chamber contains reading scrolls, a hands-on activity, and a quiz. At the end, you face Vortigrax, a dragon made of false information, and use what you have learned to defeat him.

The Journey

Three wings. Eight chambers. One dragon.

The course is organized into three wings: how AI works, how to use AI tools effectively, and how to use AI fairly and honestly. Each chamber contains three reading scrolls, one hands-on activity, and a short quiz. Completing a chamber unlocks the next one.

    Earn gold

    You earn gold for every scroll you read, every trial you complete, and every correct answer. Gold also raises your rank, from Peasant all the way up to Guardian of the Citadel.

    Collect honors

    Unlock twelve achievements, ranging from Eagle Eye on the archery range to Unscathed in the battle against the dragon.

    Print your knighthood

    After you defeat Vortigrax, the Hall of Honors creates a personalized certificate that lists your rank and gold. You can print it.

        How this site was built

        • Code: hand-written HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. No web frameworks or website builders were used. AI (Gemini) was used in aid for the complex parts and for ideas/learning concepts purposes. This is our original work.
        • Writing and AI use: AI (Gemini) helped come up with ideas for the trial, quiz, content, interface elements, and dragon battle. In the end, all the work was done by the students.
        • Art: the castle, map, characters, dragon, icons, diagrams, and certificate are original SVG, CSS, and HTML Canvas drawings made by our team. No stock or copyrighted images are used.
        • Fonts: Cinzel, Cinzel Decorative, Alegreya, Alegreya Sans, IBM Plex Mono, and Pinyon Script from Google Fonts, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
        • Sound: every sound effect is generated live with the Web Audio API. No audio files are used.
        • Privacy: progress is saved only in your own browser (localStorage). No accounts are created and no data is collected.

        Copyright & permissions

        This site contains no copyrighted text, images, or audio from other creators, so no written permissions were required.
